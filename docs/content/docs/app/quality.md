# Quality

The **Quality** tab (`/sessions/:sessionId/quality`) gathers outcome and evaluation signals for
one session after close-time pipelines finish.

## What you see

Depending on what ran for the session:

- **Outcome** — completed, escalated, abandoned, and related end states, with reasoning when
  available
- **Sentiment** — turn-level sentiment arc (jump to the proving turn on Timeline)
- **Goal lifecycle** — whether the agent fulfilled the caller’s intent
- **Topics** — classified subject labels for the session
- **Custom evaluations** — results from judges you defined under [Evals](/app/evaluations)

Fleet-level quality trends live on [Overview](/app/overview) and the agent workspace Quality
tab. Open Timeline when you need transcript or audio evidence for a score.
