# Topics

The **Topics** page (`/topics`) groups inquiries into lasting topic labels so you can see volume,
outcomes, and cost by subject as well as by agent.

## Why topics matter

After sessions close, Parlot classifies what the interaction was about. Topics compound over time:

- Reused labels show recurring demand (for example, billing disputes).
- New topic labels surface emerging issues before you define a fixed list.

Use topics to prioritize product fixes, staffing, and evaluation coverage.

## Working with topic groups

On Topics you can:

- Browse topic and subtopic volume for the organization
- Merge or manage groups when labels should be combined (as the UI allows)
- Drill into filtered [Sessions](/app/sessions) for a topic

Per-agent topic detail also appears in the [agent workspace](/app/agents) Topics tab, where the
catalog is scoped to one deployment.

## Related views

- [Quality](/app/quality) and [Overview](/app/overview) for outcome context
- [Cost](/app/cost) when you care about spend by subject
- [Evaluations](/app/evaluations) when you want judges focused on a topic cohort
