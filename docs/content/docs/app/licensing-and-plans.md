# Licensing and plans

This page summarizes what you can use without a paid Cloud subscription, and what Parlot Cloud plans unlock in the product.

## SDK instrumentation

The open-source software development kits (SDKs) that instrument LiveKit Agents and LangGraph are licensed under the **MIT** license. See the [SDK documentation](/sdk).

## Platform core (Business Source License 1.1)

The Parlot analysis platform (dashboard, application programming interface (API), ingestion, and evaluation services) is licensed under the **Business Source License 1.1 (BSL 1.1)**.

Under the Additional Use Grant:

- Free production use is capped at **4,000 conversational turns per month** for your own agents and agents you operate for clients.
- Above that cap, or to offer Parlot as a hosted service to third parties, you need a commercial arrangement with Parlot.
- Each version converts to **Apache License 2.0** four years after its first public release.

Full legal text ships with the platform repository `LICENSE`. For commercial questions, visit [parlot.ai](https://parlot.ai).

## Parlot Cloud plans

Hosted **Parlot Cloud** manages authentication, billing, and entitlements in the product. Open **Settings → Billing** to see your current plan and turn usage.

Plans build on the Free tier. Exact numbers can change; treat Settings → Billing and in-app entitlements as authoritative. Conceptually:

| Plan | Conversational turns (illustrative) | Highlights |
|------|-------------------------------------|------------|
| **Free** | 4,000 / month | Short retention, limited recordings and exports, limited custom evaluations, no multi-organization creation |
| **Starter** | Higher monthly turns | Longer retention, more recordings, multi-organization creation, event-driven evaluations, webhook alerting |
| **Growth** | Higher still | Broader retention and unlimited recordings on typical catalogs, unlimited custom evaluations |
| **Scale** | Large monthly turns | HIPAA-oriented export flags where offered, extended retention |
| **Enterprise** | Custom / unlimited in catalog | Highest retention and governance options; contact sales |

Paid tiers commonly unlock:

- Higher turn and recording allowances
- More internal seats and client viewers
- Multi-organization creation for a user or account
- Event-driven custom evaluations and webhook alerting
- Expanded data export quotas
