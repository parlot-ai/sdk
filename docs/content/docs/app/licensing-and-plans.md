# Licensing and plans

This page summarizes what Free includes, and what Parlot Cloud plans unlock in the product.

## SDK instrumentation

The open-source SDKs that instrument LiveKit Agents and LangGraph are licensed under the **MIT**
license. See the [SDK documentation](/sdk).

## Platform core (Business Source License 1.1)

The Parlot analysis platform (dashboard, API, ingestion, and evaluation services) is licensed
under the **Business Source License 1.1 (BSL 1.1)**.

Under the Additional Use Grant:

- Free production use is capped at **4,000 conversational turns per month** for your own agents
  and agents you operate for clients.
- Above that cap, or to offer Parlot as a hosted service to third parties, you need a commercial
  arrangement with Parlot.
- Each version converts to **Apache License 2.0** four years after its first public release.

Full legal text ships with the platform repository `LICENSE`. For commercial questions, visit
[parlot.ai](https://parlot.ai).

## Parlot Cloud plans

Hosted **Parlot Cloud** manages authentication, billing, and entitlements in the product. Open
**Settings → Plan & billing** to see your current plan and turn usage.

Plans build on the Free tier. Exact entitlements can change; treat Settings → Plan & billing and
in-app entitlements as authoritative. Typical included turns:

| Plan | Conversational turns (typical) | Highlights |
|------|--------------------------------|------------|
| **Free** | 4,000 / month | Short retention, limited recordings and exports, limited custom evaluations, single organization |
| **Starter** | 20,000 / month | Longer retention, more recordings, multi-organization creation, event-driven evaluations, webhook alerting |
| **Growth** | 100,000 / month | Broader retention and unlimited recordings on typical catalogs, unlimited custom evaluations |
| **Scale** | 400,000 / month | Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)–oriented export flags where offered, extended retention |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Highest retention and governance options; contact sales |

Paid tiers commonly unlock:

- Higher turn and recording allowances
- More internal seats and client viewers
- Multi-organization creation for a user or account
- Event-driven custom evaluations and webhook alerting
- Expanded data export quotas
